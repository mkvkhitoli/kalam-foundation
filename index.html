✦ Hamara Sankalp, Ek Behtar Kal

Education, sports and awareness for a better Khitoli.

Started as Kalam Classes on Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam’s birthday in 2016, Kalam Foundation works for education, humanity, nature and women empowerment — with free coaching for needy students, sports for all, and drives for a cleaner, safer, digitally aware village.

Who we are

From a small classroom to a village movement

On 15 October 2016, three young graduates from Khitoli started Kalam Classes near the village post office to help local students study for school and competitive exams. In 2020 it grew into Kalam Foundation — and today more than 100 volunteers carry the work forward.

What we do

Our focus areas

We work where it matters most to our village — learning, health, nature and standing together.

Education for every child

Free coaching for students from poor families — all school classes, all subjects.

Sports for all

Sports build confidence and discipline. We give boys and girls a field to play on.

Digital & cyber awareness

Helping students and families use phones and the internet safely and smartly.

Health & hygiene

Practical health support, organised with local volunteers.

Nature & cleanliness

A cleaner village and a greener area, one drive at a time.

Help in need

When a family faces hardship, our members come together — with money and with hands.
