About us

Who we are

Kalam Foundation is a registered non-profit working for health, dignity and opportunity for underserved families — through direct, local, volunteer-led action.

What we do

Our programs

Focused, on-the-ground initiatives that respond to real needs in our community.

Health camps

Free check-ups, eye and dental camps, blood donation drives and medicine distribution.

Relief & ration drives

Food kits, blankets and essentials for families during floods, winters and emergencies.

Awareness campaigns

Sessions on hygiene, nutrition, de-addiction, government schemes and citizen rights.
