Our programs

Education, sports, awareness, health, nature and help in need — all run by volunteers from our own community.

Education for every child

Free coaching for students from poor families — all school classes, all subjects.

  • Free classes & coaching
  • Career guidance
  • Small library
  • Digital lab & AI classes
  • Scholarships for very poor students

Sports for all

Sports build confidence and discipline. We give boys and girls a field to play on.

  • Fit Khitouli Marathon
  • Cricket tournaments
  • Women’s cricket tournament
  • Girls’ sports for empowerment

Digital & cyber awareness

Helping students and families use phones and the internet safely and smartly.

  • Digital literacy sessions
  • Cyber safety awareness
  • AI & computer basics

Health & hygiene

Practical health support, organised with local volunteers.

  • Blood test camps
  • Blood donation drives
  • Hygiene awareness for teenage girls by women volunteers

Nature & cleanliness

A cleaner village and a greener area, one drive at a time.

  • 500+ trees planted and cared for
  • Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve clean-up (Khitoli & Tala zones)
  • Water dam & village cleanliness drives
  • Civic sense awareness

Help in need

When a family faces hardship, our members come together — with money and with hands.

  • Support for families in difficulty
  • Help with family occasions for the needy
  • Standing together in joy and sorrow
Every year

Foundation Day — 15 October

Every year on Dr. Kalam’s birth anniversary we hold a cultural program for children, a personality development session, and invite people who have achieved something special in life to motivate our students.
